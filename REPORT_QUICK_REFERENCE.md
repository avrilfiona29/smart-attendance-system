## Algorithms Used

1. **Face Detection**: Histogram of Oriented Gradients (HOG)
2. **Face Recognition**: Deep learning-based face embeddings (128-dimensional)
3. **Face Comparison**: Euclidean distance with tolerance threshold
4. **Image Processing**: OpenCV operations
